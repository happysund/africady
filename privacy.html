Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Who We Are

Africa Dynamite Organisation (ADO) is a community-driven initiative dedicated to supporting children's education across Africa. We can be reached at africadynamite25@gmail.com.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide, including: your name and email address when you subscribe to updates or contact us; your email address and name when making a donation via PayPal (processed by PayPal, not stored by us); and any information you submit through our contact form.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to: send you updates about our programs and impact (if you subscribed); respond to your enquiries; and process and acknowledge donations. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Donations & Payments

All financial transactions are processed by PayPal. We do not collect or store payment card information. Please refer to PayPal's privacy policy for details on how they handle your financial data.

5. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly. We send no more than 2 emails per month.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic session cookies for functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Our website is served over HTTPS (via Cloudflare) to encrypt data in transit.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Contact us at info@africadynamite.org with any such requests.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at info@africadynamite.org.
