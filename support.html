More Questions About Giving

Are donations tax-deductible?

As we are in the process of formal registration, tax-deductibility will be available once we receive official nonprofit status. We maintain detailed records for all donations.

Can I donate in currencies other than USD?

Yes! PayPal automatically converts your local currency to USD. For bank transfers in other currencies, please contact us for specific instructions.

How much of my donation goes directly to programs?

We strive to maximize program impact. Currently, the majority of funds go directly to educational support, with minimal operational costs as we operate primarily through volunteers.

Can I set up recurring monthly donations?

Yes! Contact us at africadynamite25@gmail.com to set up automatic monthly giving through PayPal or bank transfer.

Will I receive updates about my donation's impact?

Absolutely! All donors receive regular updates about program activities, success stories, and the specific impact of their contributions.

Can I donate educational materials instead of money?

Yes! We accept in-kind donations of books, school supplies, and educational materials. Contact us to coordinate donation details and shipping.
