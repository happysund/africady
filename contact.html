Frequently Asked Questions

How can I Give to support a specific child?

Visit our Children page to see profiles of children in need. Each profile has a direct donation button for targeted support.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes! We have many remote volunteer opportunities in communications, research, fundraising, and administration. Visit our Get Involved page to learn more.

How do you ensure transparency in fund usage?

We maintain strict financial accountability, provide regular updates to donors, and document all program activities. We're working toward formal registration to further strengthen our governance.

Can my organization partner with ADO?

Absolutely! We welcome partnerships with schools, NGOs, businesses, and community groups. Contact us to discuss collaboration opportunities.

In which countries does ADO operate?

We currently support children across multiple African countries including Nigeria, Kenya, Somalia, Malawi, Niger, and Tanzania, with plans to expand to more regions.

How can I stay updated on ADO's work?

Follow us on social media and check our website regularly for updates on programs, success stories, and opportunities to get involved.
