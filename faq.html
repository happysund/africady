About ADO

What is Africa Dynamite Organisation?
Africa Dynamite Organisation (ADO) is a community-driven non-profit initiative founded in 2025 by a team of committed individuals working across Africa and the African diaspora. We focus on providing quality education, safety, and opportunities to children across Africa who face barriers like poverty, conflict, and lack of infrastructure.
Is ADO a registered charity?
ADO is currently operating as a community initiative and actively working toward formal registration as a recognized non-profit organization. Throughout this process, we maintain full transparency in our operations and follow ethical practices in all our activities. All donations are tracked and documented, and we provide regular updates to our supporters.
Where does ADO operate?
We currently operate across 12 African countries, partnering with local schools and communities. Our team includes members working both on the ground in Africa and in the diaspora, allowing us to leverage local knowledge and international support to maximize our impact.

Donations & Funding

How do I know my donation is being used effectively?
Transparency is central to our mission. All donations are tracked using secure systems, and we maintain detailed records of every contribution. Donors receive regular updates via email showing exactly how their money is being used, including photos, stories, and impact metrics. You can also request a detailed breakdown of how your specific donation was allocated.
What percentage of my donation goes directly to programs?
As a community-driven initiative, we operate with minimal overhead. Approximately 85-90% of donations go directly to program activities (educational materials, school supplies, scholarships, teacher training). The remaining 10-15% covers essential operational costs like payment processing, communication tools, and program coordination.
Can I donate in currencies other than USD?
Currently, our PayPal system accepts USD donations. However, PayPal automatically converts many major currencies. If you prefer to donate in a different currency or via bank transfer, please contact us at africadynamite25@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +237676092268, and we'll arrange an alternative method.
Can I set up a monthly recurring donation?
Yes! Monthly donations help us plan long-term and provide consistent support to children. You can set up recurring donations through PayPal, or contact us directly to arrange a monthly giving plan. We offer different tiers: $10/month (School Supplies Champion), $25/month (Education Champion), and $50/month (Future Leader Champion).
Is my donation tax-deductible?
As we are currently working toward formal charitable registration, we cannot yet provide tax receipts for deductions. However, once our registration is complete, we will be able to issue tax receipts to eligible donors. Please check back with us or subscribe to our newsletter for updates on our registration status.

Programs & Impact

What programs does ADO run?
We focus on three main areas: (1) Foundational Learning - supporting literacy and numeracy programs; (2) Education in Emergencies - providing rapid educational support for children in conflict or crisis; (3) Learning Recovery - helping children return to school and catch up after disruptions. We also distribute books, provide school supplies, and offer teacher training.
How do you select children for support?
We work closely with local schools, community leaders, and teachers to identify children who face the greatest barriers to education. Priority is given to children from families experiencing extreme poverty, those affected by conflict or displacement, orphans, and girls in communities where gender discrimination limits educational access.
Can I sponsor a specific child?
Yes! Our child sponsorship program allows you to support a specific child's education journey. Sponsors receive regular updates, photos, and progress reports about their sponsored child. You can browse available children on our Children page and select a child to sponsor. Sponsorship typically covers school fees, uniforms, supplies, and meals.
How many children has ADO helped so far?
Since our founding in 2025, we've supported over 150 children across 12 countries, partnered with 20 schools, and distributed over 5,000 books. We're growing rapidly and aim to reach 500 children by the end of 2025. You can see our current impact metrics on our homepage.

Getting Involved

Can I volunteer with ADO?
Absolutely! We welcome volunteers with various skills - from teaching and program coordination to marketing, fundraising, and web design. Both on-ground volunteers in Africa and remote volunteers from the diaspora are needed. Visit our Get Involved page or contact us to discuss volunteer opportunities that match your skills and availability.
Can I visit the schools or children you support?
Yes, we welcome visits from donors and supporters who want to see our work firsthand! However, we require advance notice to coordinate with local partners and ensure child safety protocols are followed. Please contact us at least 4-6 weeks before your planned visit, and we'll help arrange a meaningful and respectful visit experience.
How can my school/organization partner with ADO?
We're always looking for partnerships with schools, community organizations, businesses, and faith groups. Partnerships can include fundraising drives, book donations, volunteer programs, or awareness campaigns. Contact us at africadynamite25@gmail.com to discuss partnership opportunities that align with both our missions.
Can I donate books or supplies instead of money?
Yes! We accept donations of new or gently-used educational materials, books, school supplies, and uniforms. However, due to shipping costs and logistics, we often recommend monetary donations which allow us to purchase materials locally (supporting local economies) and ensure items match specific needs. Contact us to discuss in-kind donations.

Contact & Communication

How can I contact ADO?
You can reach us via email at info@africadynamite.org (we respond within 24-48 hours) or WhatsApp at +237676092268 for immediate questions. You can also use the contact form on our Contact page or reach out via our social media channels.
How often will I receive updates about my donation?
General donors receive quarterly updates via our newsletter, featuring success stories, program updates, and financial summaries. Child sponsors receive more frequent updates (typically monthly or bi-monthly) with specific information about their sponsored child's progress. You can also subscribe to our newsletter for regular updates even if you haven't donated yet.
How do I update my contact information or donation preferences?
Please email us at info@africadynamite.org with your updated information, or contact us via WhatsApp. We'll update our records promptly and confirm the changes with you.
