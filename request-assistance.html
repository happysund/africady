Request Assistance

If you are a student or pupil in need of support, fill out the form below and our team will follow up with you.

How This Works

We know that reaching out for help isn't always easy. This form allows students and their families to formally request assistance from Africa Dynamite Organisation — whether for school fees, enrollment support, learning materials, tutoring, or another need. Our team reviews every submission personally.

📝

Fill the Form

Share your details and describe your situation below.

👀

We Review It

Our team reviews every request and verifies the details.

📞

We Follow Up

We reach out using the WhatsApp number or email you provide.

Note for students: Please make sure a parent or guardian is aware that you are submitting this request — there's a checkbox in the form to confirm this.

Your privacy matters: Any photo or personal information you share is used only by our team to verify and process your request. It will never be shared publicly or with third parties without the guardian's written permission.

Prefer to reach us directly instead? You can still message us on WhatsApp or use our contact page.
