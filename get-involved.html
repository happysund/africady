Volunteer & Get Involved

Be part of a movement supporting education across Africa.

Why Get Involved?

Africa Dynamite Organisation (ADO) is driven by people who believe every child deserves access to quality education. By getting involved, you help create real opportunities and lasting change for children and communities across the continent.

💡

Make Real Impact

Your time, skills, and passion directly transform children's lives and create lasting educational opportunities.

🌍

Join a Movement

Connect with like-minded individuals across Africa and the diaspora working toward educational equity.

🎯

Use Your Skills

Whether you're a teacher, designer, or organizer, your unique talents can support our mission in meaningful ways.

Ways You Can Get Involved

🤝

Volunteer Your Time

Support our work remotely or on the ground by assisting with outreach, coordination, research, communications, or community engagement.

  • Remote or in-person opportunities
  • Flexible time commitments
  • Various skill levels welcome
  • Make a difference from anywhere
🌍

Become a Country Coordinator

Help identify children in need, liaise with local schools, and support program monitoring within your community.

  • Lead programs in your region
  • Build local partnerships
  • Monitor educational outcomes
  • Community-based leadership
📣

Advocate & Fundraise

Raise awareness, organize small fundraisers, or share our mission within your network to support children's education.

  • Social media advocacy
  • Community fundraising events
  • Corporate partnerships
  • Educational awareness campaigns
🏫

Partner With Us

Schools, community groups, NGOs, and businesses can collaborate with us to expand education access and resources.

  • Institutional partnerships
  • Resource sharing programs
  • Collaborative initiatives
  • Corporate social responsibility
🎓

Skills-Based Support

Contribute professional skills such as design, IT, translation, teaching, or administration to strengthen our capacity.

  • Graphic design & branding
  • Web development & IT
  • Translation & language support
  • Program management & admin
💛

Sponsor a Child's Education

Provide targeted support to help a child stay in school and build a better future through education.

  • Direct impact on one child
  • Regular progress updates
  • Tax-deductible contributions
  • Transform a life permanently
