Who We Are

The African Dynamite Organization (ADO), founded in 2025 by individuals from the African diaspora and some African countries, is deeply committed to championing child education in Africa. Our core mission is to ensure that every child, regardless of their background, has access to quality education.

Alarmingly, around 46 million school-aged children in Africa are currently out of school. This puts them at severe risk of exploitation, including forced child marriage, child labor, and recruitment into armed groups.

At ADO, we firmly believe that investing in every child's education is crucial for their well-being and the development of the entire African continent. We aim to decrease learning poverty rates by focusing on foundational learning, providing high-quality education in emergencies, and helping children recover lost learning.

Current Status

ADO is currently operating as an initiative group and is in the process of formal registration.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To ensure every African child has access to quality education by removing barriers, building partnerships, and creating sustainable community-led solutions.

Our Vision

A future where every child in Africa can learn, grow, and reach their full potential through accessible, quality education.

Transparency & Accountability

Building trust through clear communication and ethical practices is at the heart of everything we do.

Financial Management

Every donation is tracked using secure systems. We maintain detailed records of contributions and expenditures, ensuring funds directly support our educational programs.

Registration Process

ADO is actively pursuing formal registration as a non-profit organization. We operate with full transparency during this process and follow ethical guidelines in all our activities.

Regular Reporting

Donors and partners receive periodic updates on program outcomes, financial usage, and the impact of their support on children's lives across Africa.

Meet Our Leadership Team

Edwin Founwih

Founder & Executive Director
Edwin Founwih is the Founder and Executive Director of Africa Dynamite Organisation (ADO), established in 2025 with a clear mission: to ensure that no African child is denied the right to quality education. Driven by first-hand understanding of the barriers children across Africa face, Edwin built ADO from the ground up as a community-centred initiative — one that prioritises transparency, dignity, and long-term impact over quick fixes. He leads the organisation's overall strategy, partnerships, and growth, while keeping the needs of children at the core of every decision. Edwin believes that education is the most powerful tool for transforming Africa from within, and that lasting change must be driven by Africans, for Africans.

Larinete M.

Co-Founder & Operations Director
Enda Mbuya is the Co-Founder and Operations Director of Africa Dynamite Organisation (ADO), bringing a wealth of hands-on experience in child welfare and project delivery across Cameroon. Before co-founding ADO, Enda served as a Child Protection Officer, working directly with vulnerable children and families to ensure their safety, rights, and access to essential services. At ADO, Enda oversees the day-to-day operations of the organisation, ensuring that every program runs efficiently, transparently, and with the children's best interests at heart.

Hanna Mbuh

Program Manager
Hanna Mbuh is a results-driven professional and trained pedagogue with extensive experience in the implementation and oversight of educational programs across Africa. As Program Manager at Africa Dynamite Organisation, she is responsible for the day-to-day coordination of ADO's initiatives, ensuring every project is delivered efficiently and remains firmly focused on its core goal: getting children into school and keeping them there.

Collette Earliness

Collette Earliness is the Community Outreach Coordinator at Africa Dynamite Organisation, responsible for building and maintaining strong relationships between ADO and the communities it serves.
