The Education Crisis in Africa

Across Africa, more than 46 million school-aged children are currently out of school. Barriers like poverty, conflict, gender discrimination, and lack of infrastructure deny millions the chance to learn and build a better future.

Who We Are

Africa Dynamite Organisation (ADO) is a community-driven, non-profit initiative founded in 2025 by a team of committed individuals working across Africa and the African diaspora. We believe that education is the foundation for lasting change, and every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive regardless of their circumstances.

What We Do

📘 Foundational Learning

Supporting literacy and numeracy programs to build strong educational foundations for children across Africa.

🚨 Education in Emergencies

Providing rapid educational support for children affected by conflict, displacement, and crisis situations.

🔄 Learning Recovery

Helping children return to school and catch up after prolonged disruptions to their education.

Our Impact

Through community partnerships and dedicated support, we're helping children overcome barriers and access the education they deserve. Every donation creates lasting change.
